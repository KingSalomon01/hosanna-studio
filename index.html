Hosanna Studio — synchronized lyric videos for the HOSANNA channel

Hosanna Studio is a private desktop application used only by its operator to produce lyric videos of Christian songs for the YouTube channel HOSANNA (@hosanna-digital) and the operator's own channels. It runs entirely on the operator's computer. It is not distributed, not sold, and has no user accounts.

Hosanna Studio uses YouTube API Services.

What it does

  • Imports a song for which the operator holds the rights, together with its lyrics.
  • Synchronizes every sung line with the voice, using speech recognition that runs locally on the computer (no audio is sent to any online service).
  • Renders a full-length lyric video (1080p) and proposes several thumbnails (1280 × 720) that the operator can choose, edit, regenerate and download.
  • After the operator's approval, uploads the video to the operator's own YouTube channel as a private video, with its thumbnail, lyrics captions and, optionally, a playlist.
  • Reads the view statistics of the videos it uploaded, to measure their performance.

Hosanna Studio only acts on the channel the operator connected and locked. It never accesses other users' channels or data, and never makes a video public by itself.
