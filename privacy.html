1. Who we are

2. YouTube API Services

Hosanna Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Hosanna Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data processed by YouTube and Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access, collect and store

With the explicit authorization of the account owner (Google OAuth 2.0), Hosanna Studio accesses:

Permission (scope)Data accessed
youtube.readonlyIdentity of the connected channel (ID, name, handle), its playlists, its recent uploads, and the status and public counters (views, likes, comments) of the videos uploaded by Hosanna Studio.
youtube.uploadUpload of videos and their custom thumbnails to the connected channel.
youtube.force-sslAdding an uploaded video to a playlist of the channel, uploading its lyrics captions, and changing the visibility schedule of a video uploaded by Hosanna Studio.
yt-analytics.readonly (optional)Views, watch time, average percentage viewed and subscribers gained for the videos uploaded by Hosanna Studio, at day 2, 7 and 30 after release.

Hosanna Studio does not access the user's email address, contacts, comments, private messages, or any other channel than the one the operator connected and confirmed.

The application also stores the files the operator imports (songs, lyrics, images) and the videos and thumbnails it produces. These files never leave the computer, except the videos, thumbnails and captions that the operator approves for upload to their own channel.

4. How information is used

  • to verify that uploads go to the channel the operator locked, and never to another one;
  • to upload approved videos as private, with their thumbnail, captions and playlist;
  • to detect an interrupted upload and avoid duplicate videos;
  • to show the operator the performance of their own videos.

Hosanna Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide these user-facing features. It is not used for advertising, not used to train artificial intelligence models, and not read by any person other than the operator.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any user data or API data to any third party, internal or external. The only recipient of uploaded content is YouTube itself, on the operator's own channel. Hosanna Studio does not display third-party content or advertising.

6. Storage, cookies and security

  • All data is stored locally on the operator's computer, in a local database. No server operated by Hosanna Studio receives it.
  • The OAuth access token is stored encrypted in the Windows Credential Manager, never in plain text.
  • Hosanna Studio's interface runs on the computer itself (127.0.0.1) and stores a session cookie in the local browser solely to protect its own forms. No tracking or advertising cookie is used.
  • Statistics obtained from YouTube API Services are kept only while the authorization remains valid; Hosanna Studio checks that authorization at least every 30 days. Other API data (channel identity, video status) is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. After a revocation, the operator deletes the stored token and all data obtained from YouTube API Services within 7 calendar days (and in any case within 30 days for a revocation made on Google's page). Any request for deletion sent to [CONTACT EMAIL] is handled as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days.
